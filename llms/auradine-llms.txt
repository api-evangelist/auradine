# Velaura AI

> Velaura AI, Inc., formerly Auradine, builds ultra-low-power compute solutions for data center, edge, and physical AI applications. Its Titan Core silicon design and IP platform helps AI compute platforms reduce power requirements while maintaining performance.

## Core pages

- [Homepage](https://velaura.ai/): Overview of Velaura AI and its ultra-low-power compute focus for cloud, edge, and physical AI.
- [Titan Core - Low Power AI Compute](https://velaura.ai/low-power-ai-compute/): Silicon design and IP platform for ultra-low-power AI compute. Public page claims include approximately 2-4x reduction in power used for arithmetic operations, 250W-500W reduction on a 1,000W XPU chip, and $650-$1,300 per chip in estimated three-year power savings, excluding cooling and infrastructure.
- [Teraflux Bitcoin Mining Solutions](https://velaura.ai/products/): High-efficiency Bitcoin mining hardware, ASICs, containers, and fleet management software.
